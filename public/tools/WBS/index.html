About WBS

35 Years of Getting Things Done

Founded in Dublin in 1989 by Reginald Wingette, WBS has grown from a two-person consultancy into one of Ireland's most versatile design and manufacturing groups. We operate across three specialist divisions from our offices in Dublin, Cork, and Wicklow.

We pride ourselves on a can-do culture, an honest approach to client relationships, and the kind of practical experience that only comes from three decades in the industry.
