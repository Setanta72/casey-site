Why Most Organisations Aren't Ready for Design Transformation

The gap between design ambition and organisational readiness is not merely a resourcing problem — it is, at its root, an epistemic one. Organisations that commission design without first developing the cognitive and structural conditions to absorb it are not making a design investment; they are making a very expensive mistake. In this essay, I argue that design readiness is a prerequisite, not an outcome, of design engagement — and that our profession has been complicit in obscuring this.
