# Stream 1 Brief — Standalone Process & Artifact Showcase (`KUMIKO-TRUSS V6.2`)

## Goal
Stand up a **dedicated, self-contained web showcase** (`index.html` + compressed local `assets/`, ready for optional Cloudinary media hosting later) that tells the authentic, unvarnished engineering and design story of **KUMIKO-TRUSS** — completely separated from the broader `desk_lamp_studio` addendum.

## Core Narrative Arc
1. **Hero & Holding Slots (`V6.2` Final Birch Artifact):** Clean editorial hero framing the artifact, with dedicated holding cards ready for 1-drop replacement once the finished Baltic birch ply build is oiled and photographed in `../00_v6_birch_reference/photos_birch_hero/`.
2. **Design Evolution (V1 → V6.2):** Concept sheets → 4 Blender studio variations → V5 cutaway & formal mechanical audit → V6 FreeCAD parametric B-Rep (`79` parts, `16/16` zero-overlap checks, `FoS 2.05–4.09`).
3. **Autonomous Machine-Ready Outputs:** ISO A3 technical drawings, CNC `12 mm` pocket-depth milling map, A2 Illustrator laser nest (`29` / `58` parts, `0` overlaps), and Bambu X1C sliced `.gcode.3mf` plates.
4. **Physical Prototype & Human-in-the-Loop Reality Check:** Real workshop photographs of the **MDF & PS-foam test build** alongside the **exact 6 physical discoveries** that triggered the V6.1/V6.2 iteration (crown ring clash, IKEA `LED1938R3` `24V` socket clash → `+24 mm` `FDM_01C` drop-down holder, arm clamp deflection → `6 mm` spacers, butterfly nuts → `FDM_07` scalloped covers, `FDM_08` 2-piece → 1-piece unified turret pivot, and Tier 1 CNC milling time).
5. **9-Plate Illustrated Assembly Manual:** Interactive lightbox viewer of all 9 CAD-derived IKEA/Dieter-Rams-style step plates + link to the printable PDF manual.
6. **Where Next (V7 & V8 Teaser):** Brief preview of the fastener-free Open Kit (`03_v7_open_kit/`) and Kinetic Smart Lamp (`04_v8_kinetic_smart/`).
